This week, our team worked on our final goals for our second round of pizza and playtests. My job was to improve the attacks for the first boss. 
The first commit linked below is my first boss attack I made, which comes out in waves from the boss. My second commit linked below is where I made the blue bars coming in from the left and right walls of the dungeon room. I would like in the future to make them look more like water coming in waves towards the player. I think that would be cool. During the playtesting, we got a lot of good feedback on my new elemental attack and boss attacks. For the elemental attack, players seemed to like the middle of the fight more than last time, and helping to draw the symbol. In the last round of playtesting, the middle of the fight was definitely lacking. I feel like collecting the gems helped this a lot. There were some funny moments where players would get in each other's way while trying to draw the symbol and would get hit by the boss's attack becasue of it but they were laughing instead of being mad at each other, and that was really cool to see. For the new boss attacks, our returning playtester liked them a lot more than what we had before, but him and almost all the playtesters said they were too hard to dodge. They said they were too fast, and they wanted more opportunities to try and dodge them, perhaps with breaks in them to walk through. I really liked that idea, and I think I will try to tackle that this week. For this week's gold star story, I want to shout out Reed. Almost all of the work on the second boss so far has been Reed while I have tried to refine the first boss, and Sean has focused on the UI and maze. I think it will be cool in the end with the boss charging at players.

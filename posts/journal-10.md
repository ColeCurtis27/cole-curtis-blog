This week, our team reached the goals we had set for milestone 2, and began working toward sour next playtest on the 6th. I finally added in our new elemental attack. I envisioned it somewhat like an "ultimate" attack that you have to charge up. You charge it up by walking around the arena, collecting gems. that will spawn o nthe floor. Each player has to collect their own colored gem that matches their elemental typing. Once their attack bar is ready, they can spawn the attack on the floor, which is a symbol that they have to connect the dots around it to connect it all together. Once every connection is made, the attack is sent out. It lacks a lot of visual juice currently, and also has some small bugs with multiplayer and controller support, but I'm pretty happy with it. Looking forward to the playtest, my specific tasks/goals are to fix the bugs with the elemental attack, make sure it works with controller, and then the biggest one is to add more interesting boss 0/ boss 1 attacks. I'm envisioning like waves coming out from the boss and from the side of the screen, but I don't know how visually intriguing I can make that by myself, but a lot of what I want to do revolves around game juice. I want to make the player feel something while they are running around the arena trying to collect gems, avoiding attacks, attacking the boss, and doing their own eleemntal attack / helping their teammates with theirs. Oh I don't know if I mentioned earlier or to you in class yet but the sigl/symbol that spawns on the floor can be connected together by any player. This adds another layer of collaboration, past what people are doing in the maze. Speaking of the maze, I want to give a gold star story to Sean. He has been visibly passionate about this maze idea, and when we realized we need more collaboration, he took it upon himself to add it in and now he is working on adding puzzles and player interactions to it as well.

This week, my tasks were to work towards our goal of having our first playbale demo ready next week, and my part in that was everything with the boss. This week, I accomplished getting out boss on the screen, with a working idle and attack animation. There is a timer in the boss's code that runs every five seconds and spawns the attack on screen for two seconds before resetting and restarting the timer. I have a list of coordinates that form the formation of a big red X attack pattern. I have a function that loops through each coordinate and at each coordinate spawns an area2d, a collision shape, and a colorRect. The area and collisoon shape work to dheck if the player enters the body of any part of the X. The colorRect is to show a visual indicator on the floor to the player where the attack is landing. If the player collides with the X while it is on screen, the player is despawned. This was mostly just to make sure the collisin detection worked. For our playable demo, we will need to change it to just deplete the health of the player and not instantly kill them. I will link a video below that shows all of this taking place in the main scene. I will also link below my commit of code to our repo that shows all of the boss code I developed this week.

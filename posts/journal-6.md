This week, my team and I mostly worked to prepare for the first playtesting session on Friday. For me, I wanted to add in the sound effects Cristian made for us and also set the player and boss's health and damage values for the first round of playtesting. I did accomplish both of these things, and they're reflected in the github commit I linked below. There weren't any big visual changes to show for this week. They took about as long as I thought they would, which is good, but it felt like I wasted the time I spent trying to balance values for playtesting, whcih I will touch on later. Playtesting was very interesting. We received a lot of feedback, and a lot of it was stuff we expected to hear. The game doesn't feel elemental yet, the combo is weird in it's current state, and the need for more visual polish are all things we expected to hear. There were also things we didn't expect to hear. The first of which is that the tile floor we have made the tester assume that their projectile attacks are at fixed angles, but with the controller your projectile attacks are at whatever angle you point the stick. Another thing we didn't expect was we had multiple players try to attack the boss while it was "stunned" for the combo attack. Sadly, we didn't have enough ports for controllers to test with 4 players, so for our own ease we were testing with one. Interestingly enough, all the players were able to kill both the tutorial boss and the first boss on their own pretty comfortably. We had one or two testers die, but the ones who flew through the fights flew through them. I mentioned earlier that I felt like I wasted my time tweaking values because I thought I was balancing the game pretty well for four players, then testers were able to beat the boss jsut fine by themselves. This means I was WAY off in my estimations. I'm glad this happened though, because this means I have more insight now for the next bit of balancing I do. The game didn't seem too fun yet, but we did run one playtest with two players, and that seemed more fun for our players, which gives me hope for the final product with four players. IF we refine the mechanics and add more polish and fluff to our visuals I have hope for the final product. I sitll think our team communication is working well, even though we were all sick last week.

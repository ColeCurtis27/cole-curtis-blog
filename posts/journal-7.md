This week, my team and I discussed the important next steps for our game. We decided that the elemental implemetntation is an important next step, so we needed to get in writing what this would look like. At the design doc link below, you can see our curent idea for adding ele,ental types to our game. Anotable chnage will be how combo attacks work, which was my work. I had to think of how to make the combo attack/boss stun period more engaging, so I took something I noticed from our playtesting and ran with that until I had a full idea. The thing I took from playtesting was that we noticed players tried to attack the boss while in the stun state, and that was never our intention. We liked this idea though, and I hope implementing this with our elemental typing as well will make it more fun. This week, I didn't do much actual programming work, other than adding markers to the current form of the combo attack to better indicate for playtesters and our testing where players need to stand, which is the commit I linked below. Most of my work was in the design doc, which is why I also provided that. Also, I searched for new potential asset packs for us to use for our boss rooms and boss sprites. Some feedback we have found is that our game lacks personality, and a big reason for that is that we are using borrowed assets, so I'm hoping using a larger variety will make it look more like it's not all the same asset pack. Our tema communication is still good, as we do checkups in our discord chat fairly regularly.

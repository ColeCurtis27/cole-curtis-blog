This week, our team discussed needing some sort of visuals added to the game, so instead of focusing on improving the boss and it's attacks, this week I wanted to add health bars on the screen for our player and boss. Along with this, I also have the hurt and die animations playing for the boss and players when they are hit or their hp hits zero. I think this adds a good layer of visual feedback for our players. It is not perfect in its current state though. Currently, the player is able to hit the boss, and the way I have the boss's state machine set up, after it enters the hurt state when it gets hit, it next goes to the idle state. This littcle short cycle is enough time for the player to atack over and over again and ahev the boss stuck in a loop where it can't move until it runs out of HP and dies. My idea to comabt this is add some sort of timer in the player's attack function that prevents the player from being able to attack over and over again. I will probably test it first with 3-5 seconds and shift the timer from there if I find it to be too short or too long. My predicted time for this was probably a bit off, but not as bad as it has been before. It took only a little longer than I anticipated it would, so hopefully that means I'm getting better at predicting the time something will take. Either that or I'm just underestimating myself as a programmer. I still think our progress as a team is going well in our discord group chat. We keep each other updated, send reminders, and tell each other what our plans of work are for the week. We need to be better though at the speed we work at. Our first milestone is this Friday, and we still have a lot of work to do.

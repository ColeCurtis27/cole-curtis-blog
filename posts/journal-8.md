Well, this was an interesting week for me and my team. I had a plan for a new combo/team attack that I talked about last week, but when I was trying to add it, I decided it didn't make much sense and I decided to scrap it after talking to my team. This sadly took up a majority of my time during the week, so the only meaningful change I made this week was recoloring our first boss sprites to make it water themed. I also recolored the dungeon floor, but this morning we found a new tileset that we like more for our dungeon that we want to use, so I'm going to redo it. The commit below shows these changes I made. I recolored the sprites using aesprite, which I had never used before. It was actually pretty fun, and I might even try making sprite assets in the future for us, but for now it is useful to recolor sprites for our needs. Also a lot of time this week I spent tryign to look for other asset packs with sprite sheets for our characters or bosses. I found some potential assets for future bosss, but nothing for our characters. Today after class my team and I talekd about our goals for milestone 2, which we put in the document linked below. We came up with a new idea for a special attack that players can do in the fight, where players have to collect gems/charges to charge an attack, and then they have to draw a pattern on the floor. It is confusing to explain, but I hope when I implement it it'll make more sense. We hope this will be fun and engaging for our players to have more things to do in the middle of the boss fight, and then they will have to work together more in the maze and solving the puzzles in there between fights. For our character sprites, since we couldn't fight any other character sprites better suited for elemental typing, we are taking more inspiration from Castle Crashers and are going to recolor our wizard sprite to 4 different colors, representing the 4 elements we want in our game. In our playtesting also, players said that the wizard was their favorite character. This will also change all players into projectile attacks instead of the knight having a melee, becasue we also receievd feedback that it didn't feel good to play with this. This week, my work took a lot longer than I anticipiated, and most of it was wasted, so going forward I need to be a lot better with my time management. Our team communication is still good. I know we don't post in the public channel, but we do have a private group chat where we talk almost daily and update each other. I've never personally had a problem with contacting my team. Going forward we will all be working towards our milestone two goals, and I will be taking on the new elemental attack for the players and also recoloring our sprites.
